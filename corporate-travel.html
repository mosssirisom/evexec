FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you invoice our company rather than the passenger paying?

Yes. Tell us when you request a quote and we will agree invoicing with you before the first journey.

Do you provide executive airport transfers?

Yes. Our airport transfers use the same fixed prices for business travellers, from £90 to Manchester Airport, with flight tracking on the way home.

Can we book for a visitor who is not on our staff?

Yes. Book in your company’s name and give us the passenger’s name and mobile number so the driver can reach them.

Do you cover journeys outside the Fylde Coast?

Yes. We quote for meetings and site visits in Manchester, Liverpool, Leeds and beyond. Pickups outside our usual area are priced individually.
