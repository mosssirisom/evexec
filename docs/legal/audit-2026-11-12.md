# Legal audit of the terms, privacy policy and booking form (12/11/2026)

**Standing rule (owner, 12/11/2026): no solicitor is used.** The legal wording is built from official UK sources (legislation.gov.uk, the CMA, the ICO) and checked against them. This is a careful self-check, not legal advice, and it is not a guarantee. The residual risks are listed at the end so the owner knows what is judgement and what is law.

## How it was checked

Every provision below was read on the official site on 12/11/2026 (revised text, "in force" at that date). Where a source could not be read, that is stated rather than filled in from memory.

## Findings

| # | Rule (source) | What it says | Our position | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, reg 7(3) and reg 27(2)(c) (legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134) | Passenger transport contracts are outside the information duties and the 14-day right to cancel, **except** reg 14(1)-(5) for contracts concluded online. | No cooling-off period applies to a transfer (our terms do not offer one). | OK |

If not, **the customer is not bound**. | The form said only "Request My Transfer", and the price was shown at the top of the step. | **Fixed**: tick-box now says the customer must pay if the booking is confirmed; a total-price line sits directly above the button; the button reads "Request Transfer · I Agree to Pay if Confirmed"; terms section 2 states the obligation. |

## Owner actions (things the law needs that code cannot do)

1. **ICO data protection fee.** On the text of the law, running bookings and dispatch is not clearly exempt, so most operators must pay (£52 a year for a micro business, £47 by direct debit). Check with the ICO's self-assessment at ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee, pay if it says so, and put the registration number in the business's details (or "exempt"). Each new business must do the same.
2. **Complaints about data.** Keep a simple record of each complaint, acknowledge it within 30 days, reply, and tell the person the outcome.
3. **Council conditions.** Each business should read its own council's private hire operator licence conditions for what booking records to keep and for how long.
4. **Claims must be true.** "Fully insured, hire and reward and public liability", "enhanced DBS checked", and **live flight monitoring** (the platform's automatic flight checks are currently switched off, so drivers must really be tracking flights) must be true for every business that publishes these terms.
5. **Keep the cancellation charges defensible.** If a customer challenges one, be ready to show the lost slot and costs; waive or reduce it where the slot was re-booked (the terms promise this).

## Residual risks (judgement, not law)

- **Cancellation percentages.** Whether 25% / 50% / 100% is "disproportionate" is decided case by case. They are sliding, explained, reduced on re-booking and waivable, which is what the CMA guidance points to, but no one can promise a court would agree.
- **Business-to-business liability cap.** The cap and the loss-of-profit exclusion must also be reasonable under the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977. That Act was **not** read in this audit.
- **Not covered by this audit:** package travel rules (if a transfer is ever sold inside a package), alternative dispute resolution information duties, VAT, the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 rules on pricing and reviews beyond a basic price-transparency check, and licensing language specific to London (TfL), Scotland and Northern Ireland. Check those before a business in those places uses the template.
- The CMA's full unfair-terms guidance (CMA37) could not be read as text; its plain-English summary page was used.

## Changing the terms in future (the process)

1. Edit the template in `lib/legal/terms.js` (or `privacy.js`).
2. Terms wording change = a new version: copy the current `terms.html` to `terms-vN.html` (noindex, canonical `/terms-vN`, "applied to bookings accepted from ... until version N+1"), add its route in `vercel.json` and its entries in `scripts/site/build.js`, add `BANDS` and `policyPath` entries in `lib/cancellation.js` and set `TERMS_VERSION`, mirror that in the Operator app's copy (`src/lib/email/cancellation.js`), then `npm run build:legal`.
3. Bookings keep the version they accepted. Only a privacy-policy change needs just the "Last updated" date (built by `npm run build:legal`).
4. `tests/unit/legal.test.cjs` fails if the published pages and the templates drift apart.

## Per business

A business's own terms and privacy come from `lib/legal` filled from its details (Settings, once built). Before it publishes: every item in `gaps` filled; the owner actions above done for that business; its jurisdiction chosen (England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland); and its cancellation numbers, if different from the defaults, applied to the charge calculation (`lib/cancellation.js` still uses EV Exec's numbers; see TASKS.md).
