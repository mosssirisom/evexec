# EV Exec technical SEO audit, 07/10/2026

Scope: every URL on https://www.evexec.co.uk, its routing (`vercel.json`), `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, structured data, metadata, internal links, images, performance and business-identity consistency. No design or booking-flow changes were in scope.

How it was checked:

- A local copy of the Vercel routing (same `routes` and `builds` order) was crawled from `/` and from every sitemap URL, following every `<a href>`. Each page's status, canonical, robots directives, title, description, headings, Open Graph and Twitter tags, JSON-LD, images and links were recorded.
- Live production was probed (HTTP status and final URL) for redirects, trailing slashes, `.html` URLs, mixed case, the 404 page, the bare domain and the `evexec.vercel.app` host.
- Structured data was validated against the official Schema.org vocabulary (release 29.3), plus Google's required fields per feature.
- Lighthouse 12 ran locally (simulated mobile, slow 4G). Google's PageSpeed API was over quota.
- Full-page screenshots of every page were taken at 390 px and 1366 px wide, before and after, for a pixel comparison.

Not available in this environment, so not checked: Google Search Console (impressions, queries, index coverage), real search volumes, live CrUX field data, and Google's own Rich Results Test. The general web search tool used here is not Google and returned nothing for `site:evexec.co.uk`, which says nothing reliable about Google's index.

## Health assessment (before changes)

The site was already in good technical shape. All 24 public pages returned 200 and had a unique title and description, a self-referencing canonical on www, one H1, Open Graph and Twitter tags, `lang="en-GB"` and server-rendered content (Google does not need JavaScript to see the text). The sitemap listed exactly the 24 indexable pages. There were no broken links or assets, no orphan pages and no redirect chains. Lighthouse SEO scored 100 on the pages tested.

Nothing found was blocking crawling or indexing (no P0). The issues were weaker signals, duplicates and speed.

| Area | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate URLs | `/fleet.html`, `/index.html`, `/Fleet` returned 200 (canonical tags pointed to the clean URL, so this was a soft duplicate, not an error) | P1 |
| Trailing slash | `/fleet/` returned 404 instead of redirecting | P1 |
| Domain redirect | `evexec.co.uk` → `www` has no explicit status code in Vercel (`redirectStatusCode: null`), so the code used is Vercel's default; it could not be read from here | P1 (user action) |
| Second host | `evexec.vercel.app` served the full site without `noindex` (canonicals pointed to www) | P2 |
| robots.txt | `/booking` and `/account` were disallowed and noindexed; a disallow stops Google seeing the noindex, so the URLs can still show as "Indexed, though blocked by robots.txt" | P1 |
| Favicon | 160×151 JPEG, not square; Google needs a square favicon (a multiple of 48 px) and may show a generic globe instead | P1 |
| Structured data | Business typed as `TaxiService`+`LocalBusiness` (TaxiService is a service, not a business type); defined differently on the homepage and the other pages; no `logo`; homepage carried `aggregateRating` and three `review` items with approximated dates (site shows month only) | P1 |
| Review markup | Google does not show star ratings for reviews a business publishes about itself, so the markup gained nothing, and its dates were approximations | P1 |
| Article markup | Publisher logo was the 1536×1024 banner; article page lacked `og:site_name`/`og:locale` | P2 |
| Headings | Footer column titles were `<h2>` on every page (3 extra H2s per page); homepage H1 text read "Private HireBlackpool" (no space between lines) | P2 |
| Internal links | Town pages linked to the airport pages, but the airport pages never linked back to the towns in their content | P1 |
| Performance | Render-blocking Google Fonts stylesheet (~0.9 s on slow 4G) plus two third-party connections on every page | P1 |
| Accessibility | Homepage: star-rating `div`s with `aria-label` but no role; chat send button without an accessible name | P2 |
| Images | Good alt text, dimensions, lazy loading, WebP and a high-priority hero. Hero images are 1000 px wide with a single source; a smaller variant would help low-density phones only | P2 |
| Identity | Name "EV Exec", phone 07721 070370 / +44 7721 070370 and email book@evexec.co.uk consistent everywhere; Google Business Profile linked from every page | OK |

## Plan

**P0, blocking crawling or indexing:** none found.

**P1, done in this change:**

- Redirect `/index.html`, `*.html` and trailing-slash URLs to the clean URL (308).
- `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on `/booking` and `/account`; allow them in robots.txt so Google can read the noindex.
- Square favicon set (`/favicon.ico` 32/48, 192 px PNG, 180 px Apple icon); 512 px square logo for structured data.
- One business entity on every page: `LocalBusiness`, logo, images, description, phone, email, town-level address (Blackpool, no street: service-area business), service area, hours, Google Business Profile in `sameAs` and `hasMap`, founder, airport price catalogue (as `TaxiService` offers). Rating and review markup removed (reviews stay visible on the page).
- Article markup tied to the business entity with a square logo.
- Airport pages link to all town pages in their first section, with differently worded sentences on each page.
- Fonts self-hosted: the exact Google Fonts files and declarations per page, preloaded. Removes two third-party connections and the render-blocking stylesheet.
- Footer column titles no longer H2 (identical styling); homepage H1 text spacing; two accessibility fixes.
- `noindex` header on the `evexec.vercel.app` host; `/fleet` title includes "Electric".

**P2, growth (not done; worth doing once Search Console data is in):**

- A smaller (600 px) WebP variant for hero and fleet images, for low-density phones.
- Page-specific Open Graph images (each airport or town page uses its own hero image).
- A visible link from `/fleet` and `/reviews` to `/airport-transfers` and `/prices`.
- New guides only where Search Console shows real queries with no good landing page. Candidates: early-morning flights from the Fylde; Liverpool vs Manchester from Blackpool.

**P3, authority:** see below.

## Off-site opportunities (legitimate only)

Free unless marked. Anything paid needs the owner's approval first (cost policy).

- **Search engines and maps:** Google Business Profile (exists); Bing Places for Business (can import from Google); Apple Business Connect.
- **Directories (a few good ones, not hundreds):** Yell, Thomson Local and FreeIndex free listings, with the same name, phone, website and service-area wording as the site.
- **Local tourism:** Visit Blackpool and Visit Lytham St Annes visitor guides. Listings are often part of a paid membership; check before joining.
- **Local business networks:** the regional Chamber of Commerce and local business groups (usually paid membership).
- **Partnerships:** hotels, guest houses and holiday lets in Blackpool and Lytham St Annes; wedding venues; golf clubs (visiting players flying in); employers on the Fylde business parks who send staff to airports. Ask them to list EV Exec as a recommended transfer, with a link.
- **Local press:** Blackpool Gazette and Lancashire Post. Angle: an all-electric airport transfer fleet on the Fylde Coast.
- **Reviews:** keep asking every customer for a Google review after their journey. No incentives (Google's policy forbids them).

Avoid paid link schemes, private blog networks, mass directory submissions and keyword-stuffed Business Profile names.
