Comfort on long trips

Built for motorway miles

Electric cars are at their best on a long run: quiet at speed, smooth, and with no engine noise to tire you out. Climate control, phone charging for every seat and plenty of legroom make a four-hour trip feel shorter.

Charging, planned in advance. On longer journeys we plan a short charging stop at a motorway services, which doubles as a break for coffee and the toilet. We build it into your timings and tell you when we confirm, so it is never a surprise.

Pricing

How long-distance prices work

Quoted per journey

Based on distance and timing, agreed in writing before you book.

Per car, not per person

Up to four passengers for the same price, which often beats four train tickets.

Paying

Card online, bank transfer or cash. For long journeys we may ask for some or all of the fare in advance.

How far will you travel?

Anywhere on the UK mainland. Send us the pickup and destination and we will quote.

Will we need to stop to charge the car?

On the longest journeys, yes: usually one short stop at a motorway services, planned in advance and included in the timings we give you.

Do I need to pay in advance?

For long-distance or high-value journeys we may ask for full or part payment before travel. We will tell you when we quote.
