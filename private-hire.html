How it differs from a taxi

Booked, not hailed

EV Exec is a licensed private hire service, so every journey is booked in advance. We do not sit on a rank or take street hails, which means the car you booked is not halfway through someone else’s fare when you need it.

You get a fixed price before you travel, not a meter. Your driver messages you when they set off and when they arrive, and you travel in a clean, quiet electric car rather than whatever turns up.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does private hire cost?

It depends on distance and timing, so every journey is quoted individually before you book. The price you accept is the price you pay.

Can I book a car for someone else?

Yes. Give us their name and mobile number, and we will contact them directly on the day.

Can you wait while I am at an appointment?

Yes. Tell us roughly how long you expect to be and we will include waiting or a return pickup in your quote.

Do you take same-day bookings?

If a car is free, yes. Call or WhatsApp us for anything in the next few hours.
