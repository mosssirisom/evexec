Local pickups

Fleetwood, Thornton, Cleveleys and Anchorsholme

From the Mount and the seafront in Fleetwood to Larkholme and Broadwater, and from the promenade end of Cleveleys across Thornton and Anchorsholme, we collect from every address.

The A585 is the only main road out, so for early flights we leave a little more margin than we would from central Blackpool. A slow start on the Amounderness Way should never put your flight at risk.

Public transport to any airport is a long trip from here, so it makes sense to book both legs together: out and home in one booking, with your return flight tracked. A return to Manchester is £160 for both journeys.

There is no extra charge for being further up the coast: Manchester is £90 one way, the same as from Blackpool. See all prices.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Fleetwood or Cleveleys to Manchester Airport?

£90 one way or £160 return, fixed, for up to four passengers. There is no extra charge for Fleetwood or Thornton-Cleveleys.

How long does it take to get to Manchester Airport from here?

About an hour and a quarter outside rush hour. We add time for the A585 when we set your pickup.
