Manchester Airport Parking vs Private Airport Transfer

A practical guide for travellers from Blackpool, Lytham, St Annes, Poulton and the wider Fylde Coast.

Book Your Transfer Back to Blog

If you live in Blackpool, Lytham St Annes, Poulton or anywhere else on the Fylde Coast, there are two realistic ways to get to Manchester Airport for a holiday or business trip: drive and park, or book a car to take you. This guide sets out what each one really involves, so you can decide which suits your trip.

What driving and parking really costs

The parking price is the figure most people compare, but it is not the whole cost. Add to it:

  • fuel for around 120 miles there and back
  • the M55, M6, M61 and M60, including the morning queues around Manchester
  • the shuttle bus or walk from the long-stay car park to your terminal
  • the drive home after a late or long-haul flight, when you are at your most tired

Parking prices change with the car park, the time of year and how far ahead you book, so check the airport's current price for your dates and compare it with a return transfer, which from the Fylde Coast is £160 for up to four people.

When parking makes sense

Parking is often the cheaper choice for one person on a short trip, especially if you can book a car park well in advance and are happy to drive home after you land.

When a transfer makes more sense

A transfer tends to win for two or more people, longer trips (where parking costs add up by the day), very early departures and late arrivals. You are collected from your door, dropped at departures, and on the way home your driver is tracking your flight, so a delay does not leave you paying for extra waiting.

Coming home

This is the part people underestimate. After a long flight, walking straight out to a car that is already waiting is very different from finding the shuttle bus, then the car, then driving for an hour or more.
