The route

Down the M6

Two and a half hours

About 130 miles, almost all of it on the M6. We allow extra for roadworks and the stretch around Birmingham.

Terminal drop-off

Straight to departures. No long-stay parking to book and no shuttle bus.

Shared cost

£215 covers up to four passengers, which often beats parking for a fortnight away.

Timing

When we collect you

For a long-haul flight from Birmingham we usually suggest a pickup around five and a half hours before departure, giving time for the drive and the three hours most long-haul airlines recommend at the airport. We confirm a time with your booking.

Coming home, we track your flight and adjust the pickup if it lands early or late.
