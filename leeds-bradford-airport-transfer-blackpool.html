The route

Over the Pennines

Around two hours

Roughly 75 miles. We pick the route on the day, over the tops or via the M62, depending on traffic and weather.

One terminal

Leeds Bradford has a single terminal. We drop you at the front, close to check-in.

Weather-aware

The airport sits on high ground. In winter we leave extra time and watch conditions before we set off.

Timing

Planning an early flight

Because the drive is longer, we normally collect you around four hours before a European departure. For a 7am flight that means a 3am pickup. It is an early start, but you can sleep in the back of a quiet electric car rather than drive yourself across the Pennines in the dark.

Leeds Bradford is the highest airport in England, and low cloud or fog occasionally diverts arriving flights. If your flight home is diverted, call us: we will talk through the options for collecting you from where you land.

Comfort

Two hours you will not mind

Quiet electric drive, climate control set before you get in, phone charging front and back, and space to stretch out. On a longer route like this the car makes the difference.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Blackpool to Leeds Bradford Airport?

EV Exec charges a fixed £135 one way or £250 return from anywhere on the Fylde Coast, for up to four passengers.

How long does it take to drive from Blackpool to Leeds Bradford Airport?

Around two hours, depending on route, traffic and weather. We build that into your pickup time.

What if my flight into Leeds Bradford is diverted?

Call us as soon as you know. Diversions are not common, but when they happen we will work out with you whether we can collect you from the airport you land at.
