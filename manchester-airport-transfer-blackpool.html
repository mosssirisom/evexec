The route

What to expect on the way

Around an hour

About 60 miles via the M55, M6, M61 and M60. Allow 60 to 75 minutes off-peak and longer on weekday mornings around the M60.

Straight to your terminal

Tell us your terminal when you book. We drop you outside departures, so there is no transfer bus from a car park.

Drop-off charge included

Manchester charges vehicles to drop off and pick up. That cost is already in your price.

Timing your pickup

When should we collect you?

For a European flight we usually collect you around three and a half hours before departure: an hour on the road, a buffer for the M60, and the two hours most airlines ask for at check-in. For long-haul, add another half hour. We will suggest a time when we confirm your booking, and you can always ask for earlier.

Early-morning departures are the most popular slot from the Fylde Coast. A 06:00 flight means a 02:30 pickup, and those dates fill up first, so book as soon as your flights are confirmed.

Coming home

Landing at Manchester

We follow your flight from the moment it takes off. If it lands early, we are there early; if it is delayed, we move your pickup to match at no extra charge.

When we confirm your booking we agree where to meet, and your driver messages you when they are there. Take your time through passport control and baggage reclaim. You will be back on the M56 heading home without hunting for a taxi rank or a car park ticket machine.

  • Add your inbound flight number when you book.
  • Your phone number is how the driver reaches you, so keep it on after landing.
Parking or a transfer?

Compared with driving and parking

For a week away, a transfer often costs about the same as parking, without the drive home after a long flight. We have written up a fuller comparison.

Nobody drives tired

After a red-eye home, the last thing anyone wants is 60 miles of motorway.

No shuttle buses

Off-site car parks add a bus each way. We drop you at the terminal door.

Up to four of you

One price covers the whole group, so splitting it is cheaper than it looks.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Blackpool to Manchester Airport?

With EV Exec it is a fixed £90 one way or £160 return, from anywhere in Blackpool and the Fylde Coast, for up to four passengers. The airport drop-off charge is included.

How long does it take to get from Blackpool to Manchester Airport?

Usually about an hour, or 60 to 75 minutes allowing for traffic. Weekday mornings on the M60 can add time, which we build into your pickup time.

Which terminal will you drop me at?

Whichever terminal your flight leaves from. Tell us when you book, or check your airline booking if you are not sure, and we will take you to that terminal’s departures drop-off.

Do you pick up from Manchester Airport as well?

Yes. Book a single from the airport or a return. We track your flight and meet you at the meeting point agreed when we confirm the booking.
