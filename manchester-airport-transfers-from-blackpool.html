Why Choose an Executive Airport Transfer

What actually separates an executive transfer from a standard taxi, and is it worth it for your next trip to Manchester Airport?

Travelling to Manchester Airport should feel smooth, reliable, and stress-free from the moment you leave your door. But with taxis, private hire and executive transfers all competing for the same journey, it's not always obvious which is worth the money.

At EV Exec, we run executive Manchester Airport transfers from Blackpool in our Tesla Model Y, at a fixed price agreed before you travel. Here's what that actually gets you over a standard taxi.

Why Choose A Premium Airport Transfer?

Many travellers experience the same frustrations with standard airport taxis:

  • unreliable pickup times
  • uncomfortable vehicles
  • hidden charges
  • poor communication
  • stressful early morning journeys

EV Exec was created to offer a more premium alternative.

Our executive airport transfer service focuses on reliability, comfort, professionalism and fixed pricing, without the uncertainty often associated with traditional taxi services.

Executive Tesla Travel

Our Tesla Model Y offers:

  • spacious interior comfort

  • environmentally conscious travel

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the journey to Manchester Airport take?

Most journeys from Blackpool to Manchester Airport take approximately 75–90 minutes depending on traffic conditions.

Do you monitor flights?

Yes. We monitor flight times to help ensure smooth pickups for both outbound and return journeys.

Can I pre-book a return transfer?

Absolutely. Many customers choose to secure both legs of their journey in advance.

Is your pricing fixed?

Yes. Fixed pricing helps remove uncertainty and avoids unexpected costs.
